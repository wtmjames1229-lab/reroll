FAQ

Questions

What do I need to run it?

A Mac on a recent version of macOS. It works on Apple Silicon and Intel. On Apple Silicon, macOS may ask to install Rosetta the first time — click Install.

Mac says it can't open the app. What do I do?

The first time, right-click ReRoll and choose Open. On newer macOS, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and click "Open Anyway." You only do this once.

How does the free trial work?

Your first 10 output videos are free with every feature on. After that, enter a license key to keep going.

Does it upload my videos anywhere?

No. Everything is processed on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded.

Will the videos look different?

Only slightly. Every change stays within a small range, so each version still looks like your original.

Is there a Windows version?

Not yet. Mac only for now.

How do I cancel?

Anytime, from the link in your receipt email. You keep Pro until the end of the month you paid for.

Can I use it on more than one Mac?

Your license works on one Mac at a time. Deactivate it in the app to move it to another one.

Can I get a refund?

Try the 10 free videos first. If something's broken after you subscribe, email us and we'll make it right.
